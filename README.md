## Welcome to BuildLab

BuildLab exists for the person who wants to begin, improve, or change direction—but may not yet feel ready.

You do not need to know everything before you start. You need curiosity, consistency, and the courage to keep going when something feels difficult. We believe skills are developed through small steps, honest practice, thoughtful feedback, and the support of people who are learning alongside you.

**Founded and led by:** [basan-ta](https://github.com/basan-ta)

Our goal is to help every learner move from:

```text
Curiosity → Learning → Practice → Contribution → Confidence → Growth
```

BuildLab is not only an internship repository. It is a learning hub and a team environment where new members are welcomed, guided, mentored, and encouraged to believe in their own ability to improve.

## What BuildLab provides

- Clear learning paths for each technical and product discipline.
- A friendly onboarding experience for new members.
- Mentorship, code/design/test reviews, and practical guidance.
- Safe space to ask questions, make mistakes, and try again.
- Weekly goals that turn learning into visible progress.
- Collaboration across design, engineering, QA, cloud, AI/ML, and pre-sales.
- A real product project that gives learning a meaningful purpose.
- Evidence and case studies that learners can confidently share.

Mentorship does not mean doing the work for someone. It means helping people understand the problem, make decisions, learn from feedback, and gradually become independent.

## The six-month journey

| Stage | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Months 1–2 | Build foundations, discover the product, meet the team, and learn how to learn |
| Months 3–4 | Apply skills to a complete product slice and deliver meaningful features |
| Months 5–6 | Improve quality, prepare releases, document decisions, and present your growth |

The program is measured by progress, contribution, collaboration, quality, and reflection—not by how many tutorials someone finishes.

## What we are building

The internship is centered on a real-world **Team Management Platform**. Together, learners will design, build, test, deploy, document, and improve a product with authentication, profiles, teams, tasks, permissions, dashboards, notifications, APIs, responsive interfaces, and quality practices.

The product is important—but the people building it are the heart of BuildLab.

## Our culture

We choose growth over perfection and progress over comparison.

- Ask questions early.
- Help others without making them feel small.
- Give feedback that is honest, specific, and kind.
- Share what you learned, including what failed.
- Take ownership of your next step.
- Celebrate effort, improvement, and useful contribution.
- Leave the team stronger than you found it.

If you are new, you are welcome here. Start small, introduce yourself, choose one learning goal, and ask for help whenever you need it.

> **You are not expected to be ready on day one. You are expected to keep learning—and you will not have to learn alone.**
