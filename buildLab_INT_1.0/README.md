# BuildLab INT 1.0 — learn, contribute, and grow together

This is the six-month delivery workspace for a motivated, cross-functional team. Twelve seats, four rotating teams, 24 work weeks, and one shared product give learners a place to turn knowledge into confidence.

## You belong here

BuildLab is designed for people who want to grow—not people who already know everything.

You will be guided when you are new, supported when you are stuck, challenged when you are ready, and encouraged to become independent over time. Mentors and teammates will help you understand the work, but your questions, practice, ownership, and consistency will drive your progress.

## How mentorship works

- New members receive an orientation, a first small task, and a clear point of contact.
- Mentors explain context, review decisions, and help remove blockers.
- Weekly reviews focus on learning and improvement, not embarrassment.
- Every contributor receives specific feedback and a practical next step.
- Team members support one another through pairing, documentation, and respectful review.

Mentorship is a partnership. Ask early, share honestly, try the next step, and help someone else when you can.

## Team structure

Use these exact team names every month:

```text
team_x
team_y
team_z
team_xyz
```

Membership changes each month so everyone learns from different teammates and perspectives. Each team has three members, rotates responsibility weekly, and creates two evidence-based case studies every month.

## The learning loop

```text
Learn → Practice → Build → Review → Improve → Share
```

Every week should leave you with something visible: a design decision, code change, test report, deployment improvement, customer artifact, reflection, or helpful explanation for another teammate.

## Individual contribution folders

Each named contributor has a GitHub-username folder under their primary track, with `W-01` through `W-24`. Team membership is read from the monthly roster and copied into each weekly record.

The supplied roster currently contains 11 named people. `TBD_MEMBER_12` is a temporary seat and must be replaced when the twelfth person is known.

## Our standard

We value progress over performance, curiosity over fear, and teamwork over ego. Make mistakes responsibly, learn from them openly, give feedback kindly, and leave every review or conversation clearer than it began.

> **You do not have to grow alone. BuildLab is here to help you learn, contribute, believe in yourself, and become the person you are working toward.**
